Imagattern

This is all imaginary, and this imagination is a pattern of activity. Can you decrypt it? Can you own it? Can you recreate it? Can you not just want, but want deeply and truly, and make that your agency, and then your agency, a reality?

Start back from the beginning. Imagination. You are imagining this right now, and you imagined the awareness you believe yourself to have to imagine it.

This is the first level, and it is enough. There are deeper ones — where the pattern becomes an equation, and the equations begin to speak to one another — but not yet. For now, only this: learn to read the pattern beneath your own imagining.

The practice · level one
An auditory n-back is still running underneath — numbers spoken aloud, nothing shown. What changes is what you do in the silence between them.
1
Let a stream rise. Eyes open or closed — images, sensation, motion, whatever surfaces on its own. Don't force it.
2
Find the pattern, not the details. Not the objects — the cherry, the road, the face — but the relation running through them: what repeats, what drives what, what turns into what, what mirrors or contradicts, what scales, what holds still while the rest moves. A pattern is any regularity you can name — rhythmic, causal, structural, not just a shape.
3
Bind the pattern to the number. When a number is spoken, hand it that pattern to carry.
4
A fresh pattern for each number. Keep streaming; let every new number take its own, drawn from somewhere new.
5
Meet it when it returns. When a number comes back around — N voices ago — its pattern should surface with it. That recognition is your Match.
6
See examples. Twenty worked passes across 2-, 3-, 4-, and 5-back — a pattern found in a stream, bound to its number, then regenerated and compared when the number returns. Open ↗
The number is only a hook; the pattern is the thing you actually hold. Raise N to hold more of them at once. Decrypt → bind → recognise.

Your history

Imagattern, level one. Underneath is an adaptive auditory n-back — spoken digits, the 3-second pacing and 20+N blocks of the Jaeggi et al. protocol — but the task is to stream, find patterns, and bind each one to its number. It's a stripped-down first rung of the "pattern streaming" idea discussed in the brain-training community (the deeper levels add symbolic equations and meta-mapping between patterns). The cognitive claims around these practices are unproven; explore at your own pace.

Worked passes

See it run.

Every trial does two things at once. pair — a number is heard, a stream rises, you read its pattern (the relation running through it, not its details) and bind pattern→number. ↺ regenerate — once N numbers have passed, you bring back the pattern you bound N trials ago. ⇄ compare — you set that regenerated pattern beside the one you just discerned and ask whether they belong to the same number: same ▸ Match, press; different ▸ hold. The freshly-discerned pattern is then the one carried forward, to be regenerated and compared N trials on. Each pass below is shown trial by trial; the lit squares mark the one pair that matches.
